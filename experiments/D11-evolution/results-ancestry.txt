PROSE  v1 renders a v2 `warning` block:
    <blockquote>Do not deploy on a Friday.</blockquote>  [ degraded-via-ancestry ]
    Is the result TRUE? yes -- a warning IS a blockquote. Loses emphasis, states nothing false.

COMPUTATION  `running :=[is=sum] runningsum(total)`   column = ['120.00', '120.00', '60.00']
    v2 (understands runningsum):  ['120.00', '240.00', '300.00']
    v1 degrading via ancestry  :  300.00
    Is the result TRUE? NO. v1 prints a single scalar 300.00 where the
    document asserts a per-row series. Same type, wrong value, no error.

VERDICT: ancestry fallback is sound when the ancestor's claim is IMPLIED by
the descendant's claim (subtyping). Presentation specializations satisfy this;
function specializations essentially never do. A `warning` IS-A blockquote.
A `runningsum` is NOT-A sum.
